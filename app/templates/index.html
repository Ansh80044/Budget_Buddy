1

Receipt Scanner (OCR)

Upload any receipt image. The OCR extracts the merchant, date, and amount, then auto-categorizes the expense for your review.

2

AI Expense Categorization

Automatically classifies transactions into Indian student spending categories like Food & Dining, Travel, Groceries, and Education.

3

Adaptive Budget Engine

Recommends realistic spending limits based on monthly allowance, category spending history, and savings targets.

4

Spending Prediction

Estimates expected total month-end expenditure and alerts you early if you are at risk of exceeding your budget.

5

AI Financial Coach

Ask natural-language questions about your actual data: "Where am I spending most?", "Can I afford ₹3,000 headphones?", or "How to save ₹2,000?"

6

What-If Simulator

Test hypothetical purchasing and saving decisions in real-time without modifying your actual recorded transactions.
